Why this website ?

A CV summarizes experiences. It rarely tells a story. This website was created to give space to that story, the choices, encounters and commitments that have shaped my path beyond a single page.

I am currently a second-year engineering student at IMT Atlantique, based on the Nantes campus. Throughout my studies, I have developed a strong interest in energy systems and physical sciences, driven by a desire to understand how complex technologies interact with society and the environment.

This curiosity led me to specialize in nuclear energy, a demanding and often misunderstood field that I see as a crucial lever for the future of energy production. Today, my ambition is to combine technical expertise with environmental responsibility to contribute to more sustainable energy systems.
