Horizon 2030 - Hydrogen Engine Strategy

Grondin Marine × EHEVO Motors

This project focused on assessing the economic feasibility and deployment strategy of a hydrogen internal combustion engine for maritime and industrial applications by 2030.

I contributed to market analysis, target zone selection, competitive benchmarking and economic modeling, taking into account regulatory constraints and infrastructure maturity.

Skills - market analysis · energy strategy · hydrogen systems · teamwork

Small-Scale Wind Turbine

Piggott Blade - Design & Manufacturing

This hands-on project consisted in designing and manufacturing a Piggott-type wind turbine blade, combining aerodynamic theory and practical manufacturing.

I worked on CAD modeling, geometry optimization, material selection and CNC machining, translating theoretical profiles into a functional component.

Skills - CAD · machining · aerodynamics · renewable energy

Underground House Design

Energy & Thermal Performance Study

This project explored the design of an underground house with a strong focus on thermal inertia, passive energy strategies and environmental performance.

I worked on architectural layout, energy flow analysis and ventilation and insulation concepts, aiming to reduce energy demand through design choices.

Skills - thermal analysis · energy efficiency · architectural design
