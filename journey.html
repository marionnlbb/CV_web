My Journey

2018 – High School A first encounter with engineering

A visit to the Paris Air Show sparked my interest in engineering through inspiring encounters with women engineers.

During a school visit to the Paris Air Show (Le Bourget) with the association Elles Bougent, I discovered the engineering world through meaningful exchanges with women engineers.

This day was a real turning point. For the first time, I could picture myself in a technical, demanding and meaningful profession.

From that moment on, my objective became clear: deepen my scientific knowledge to understand, design and improve complex systems.

2021 Scientific Baccalaureate

I chose a scientific curriculum to build strong foundations in physics and mathematics.

Motivated by this first spark, I naturally pursued a scientific path and obtained my scientific baccalaureate in 2021.

This choice already reflected my attraction to understanding physical phenomena and tackling intellectually demanding subjects.

2021 – 2024 Operational Reserve – French Air Force

A three-year commitment combining discipline, service and responsibility.

In July 2021, alongside my studies, I joined the operational reserve of the French Air Force on the air base of Creil, driven by a strong desire to be useful and engaged.

I took part in reservist training supervision (CIRAA), military courses (combat, shooting, cohesion), and later joined the Human Resources Office, specifically the Chancellery Bureau.

My responsibilities included editing evaluation reports and diplomas, archive management and classification, requiring rigor, organization and reliability.

2025 – Today Operational Reserve – National Gendarmerie

Continuing to serve, closer to the population.

After moving to Nantes, I could no longer continue my commitment in Creil. Wanting to remain engaged while being closer to the population, I joined the operational reserve of the National Gendarmerie.

After completing the required training (Block 1 and 2), I now serve as a brigadier-chief, supporting local units during specific missions, often during night shifts.

2021 – 2023 Preparatory Class & University Path

Exploring science through multiple academic paths.

I began my higher education in a MPSI preparatory class in Autun, attracted by both scientific rigor and physical challenge. This experience pushed me beyond my limits.

Seeking more applied industrial sciences, I then continued at Paul Sabatier University in Toulouse, studying mechanics, electromagnetism, optics and fluid mechanics.

Following a return to northern France, I completed a third year in Energy and Materials in Amiens, focusing on energy conversion, power electronics and material characterization.

2024 – Today Engineering Degree – IMT Atlantique

Choosing engineering to broaden perspectives and impact.

I joined IMT Atlantique, recognized for its expertise in energy systems. The first year confirmed my strong interest in thermodynamics and fluid mechanics.

In my second year, I chose to specialize in nuclear energy, studying nuclear physics, reactor operation, fuel cycles, waste management, propulsion systems and Monte Carlo modeling.

I became particularly interested in the link between nuclear energy, materials science and recycling, especially regarding irradiated materials and sustainability.

2024 First Industrial Experience – Automotive Garage

Discovering industry from the ground up.

During my end-of-year internship, I worked as a mechanic in an automotive garage. I carried out maintenance tasks, basic repairs, and participated in engine disassembly and reassembly.

This experience made me aware of the environmental impact of certain industrial practices, strengthening my desire to contribute to a more responsible industry.

Since 2023 Engagements & Knowledge Sharing

Teaching, leadership and collective responsibility.

Alongside my studies, I have been giving private tutoring from middle school to undergraduate level. I also served as treasurer for student associations, supervising 13 organizations and managing large-scale events.

These roles allowed me to develop leadership, financial management and coordination skills, while contributing actively to student life.

Looking Ahead Towards Sustainable Energy Systems

Combining energy, materials and environmental responsibility.

In the long term, I aim to work at the intersection of energy systems, materials science and environmental transition, particularly through life cycle assessment (LCA).

My objective is to contribute to technically robust, environmentally responsible and socially meaningful energy solutions.
