My Journey

2018 – High School

A first encounter with engineering

A visit to the Paris Air Show sparked my interest in engineering through inspiring encounters with women engineers.

During a school visit to the Paris Air Show (Le Bourget) with the association Elles Bougent, I was introduced to the world of engineering through meaningful discussions with women engineers from various technical fields.

This experience was a true turning point. For the first time, I could clearly picture myself in a technical profession that combines rigor, innovation and real-world impact.

Beyond discovering engineering disciplines, this day helped me realize that engineering could be a way to contribute concretely to society by designing, improving and understanding complex systems.

2021

Scientific Baccalaureate

Building strong foundations in physics and mathematics.

Motivated by my early interest in engineering, I pursued a scientific curriculum and obtained my baccalaureate in 2021.

2021 – 2024

Operational Reserve – French Air Force

A three-year commitment combining discipline and responsibility.

Alongside my studies, I joined the operational reserve of the French Air Force on the Creil air base.

I worked in training supervision, military courses, and later in Human Resources, where rigor and organization were essential.

2021 – 2023

Preparatory Class & University Path

Exploring science through multiple academic paths.

I studied in a MPSI preparatory class, then continued at university in mechanics, energy systems and materials science.

2023 – Today

Engineering Degree – IMT Atlantique

Choosing engineering to broaden perspectives and impact.

I joined IMT Atlantique to gain a systemic vision of energy systems. I later specialized in nuclear energy, focusing on reactors, fuel cycles and sustainability challenges.

Looking Ahead

Towards sustainable energy systems

Combining energy, materials and environmental responsibility.

My goal is to work at the intersection of energy systems, materials science and environmental transition, contributing to responsible and durable solutions.
