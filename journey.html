My Journey

2018 – High School

A first encounter with engineering

A visit to the Paris Air Show sparked my interest in engineering through inspiring encounters with women engineers.

During a school visit to the Paris Air Show (Le Bourget) with the association Elles Bougent, I discovered the engineering world through inspiring encounters with women engineers.

This day was a real turning point: for the first time, I could picture myself in a technical, demanding and meaningful profession.

From that moment on, my objective became clear: deepen my scientific knowledge to understand, design and improve complex systems.

2021

Scientific Baccalaureate

I chose a scientific curriculum to build strong foundations in physics and mathematics.

Motivated by this first spark, I naturally pursued a scientific path and obtained a scientific baccalaureate in 2021.

This choice already reflected my attraction to understanding physical phenomena and intellectually demanding disciplines.

2021 – 2024

Operational Reserve – French Air Force

A three-year commitment combining discipline, service and responsibility.

From July 2021, alongside my studies, I joined the operational reserve of the French Air Force on the Creil air base, driven by a desire to be useful and committed.

I held several roles: supervision of reservist training (CIRAA), participation in military training courses (combat, shooting, cohesion), and later integration into the Human Resources Office, specifically the Chancellery Bureau.

I was responsible for editing evaluation reports and diplomas, as well as archive management and classification, developing rigor, reliability and a strong sense of responsibility.

2025 – Today

Operational Reserve – National Gendarmerie

Continuing to serve, closer to the population.

In 2025, after moving to Nantes, I could no longer continue my engagement in Creil. Wanting to remain committed while being closer to the population, I joined the operational reserve of the National Gendarmerie.

After completing the required training blocks, I now serve as a brigadier-chief, reinforcing local units during specific missions, often at night, which I find particularly formative.

2021 – 2023

Preparatory Class & University Path

Exploring science through multiple academic paths.

I began with a MPSI preparatory class in Autun, attracted by both scientific rigor and sport. This demanding experience allowed me to push my limits.

Seeking more industrial sciences, I then pursued a reinforced degree in mechanics at Paul Sabatier University (Toulouse), studying electromagnetism, optics, solid and fluid mechanics.

After returning north for family reasons, I completed a third year in Energy and Materials in Amiens, focusing on energy conversion, power electronics and materials characterization.

2023 – Today

Engineering Degree – IMT Atlantique

Choosing engineering to broaden perspectives and impact.

Wanting an internationally oriented and system-level engineering education, I joined IMT Atlantique, recognized for its expertise in energy.

In my second year, I chose the nuclear energy specialization, studying reactor operation, fuel cycles, waste treatment, propulsion systems, Monte Carlo modeling and human factors.

What particularly stood out to me was the link between irradiated materials, recycling and sustainability.

2024

Mechanical Internship – Sax’Auto Garage

Discovering industry from the ground up.

During my end-of-year internship, I worked as a mechanic in an automotive garage, carrying out maintenance tasks, basic repairs, and participating in engine assembly and disassembly.

This immersion revealed the environmental limits of certain industrial practices and strengthened my desire to contribute to a more responsible industry.

Since 2023

Engagements & Knowledge Sharing

Sharing knowledge and leading collective projects.

Since my L3, I have been giving private tutoring from middle school to undergraduate level. I also served as treasurer for student associations, supervising 13 organizations and managing large-scale budgets and events.

These experiences strengthened my leadership, project coordination and financial management skills.

Looking Ahead

Towards Sustainable Energy Systems

Combining energy, materials and environmental responsibility.

In the long term, I aim to work in fields related to life cycle assessment (LCA), energy systems and materials, contributing to environmental transition.

My objective is to reconcile technological performance, industrial constraints and societal challenges to build sustainable energy systems.
