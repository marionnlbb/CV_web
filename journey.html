My Journey

2018 – High School

A first encounter with engineering

A visit to the Paris Air Show sparked my interest in engineering.

During a school visit to the Paris Air Show (Le Bourget) with the association Elles Bougent, I discovered engineering through inspiring encounters with women engineers.

This day marked a turning point: for the first time, I could project myself into a technical and meaningful career.

2021

Scientific Baccalaureate

Building strong foundations in physics and mathematics.

Motivated by this initial spark, I pursued a scientific curriculum and obtained my scientific baccalaureate in 2021.

2021 – 2024

Operational Reserve – French Air Force

Discipline, service and responsibility alongside my studies.

I joined the operational reserve of the French Air Force on the Creil air base. I contributed to training supervision, military courses and later worked in the Human Resources Chancellery Office.

These missions strengthened my rigor, organization and sense of responsibility.

2021 – 2023

Preparatory Class & University Path

Exploring demanding academic paths to refine my direction.

I began in a MPSI preparatory class in Autun, then continued at Paul Sabatier University in Toulouse, focusing on mechanics and physics.

I later completed a third year in Energy and Materials in Amiens, deepening my interest in energy systems and material characterization.

2024 – Today

Engineering Degree – IMT Atlantique

Developing expertise in energy systems and sustainability.

I joined IMT Atlantique and chose to specialize in nuclear energy. I study reactor physics, fuel cycles, waste management and Monte Carlo modeling.

I am particularly interested in the link between energy, materials and recycling.

Looking Ahead

Towards Sustainable Energy Systems

Combining energy, materials and environmental responsibility.

I aim to work on energy systems and materials through life cycle assessment, contributing to sustainable and socially responsible solutions.
